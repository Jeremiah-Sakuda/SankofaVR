Sankofa is an Akan word from Ghana: go back and get it. The title is borrowed; this artwork tells a different, Kenyan story.

What We Carry imagines a meeting between Wambui, a Kikuyu trader, and Naserian, a Maasai trader, before Nairobi became a city. Their words, gifts and personal relationship are fiction; the regional trade is historical context.

The painted figures, garments and scene are theatrical drafts, not authenticated portraits or dress. Cultural and language review is still pending.

You are a guest. Your hands can help, or you can watch. The women decide their agreement. Only Keep retains your visit on this headset; Forget removes that saved telling and memento.

Replay retains the saved words, recordings and resolved actions, using current staging. Questions and interruption timing are not replayed.
