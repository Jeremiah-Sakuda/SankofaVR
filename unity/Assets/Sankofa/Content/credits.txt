Sankofa borrows an Akan word from Ghana: go back and get it. The Enkare Nairobi collection imagines everyday meetings around the 1850s, alongside marked historical context. The people and their relationships are fictional; these tellings do not establish anyone's ancestry. The earlier What We Carry comparison features fictional Kikuyu and Maasai traders. Cultural and language review remain pending.

Illustrations: cast poses, scenery, river paintings, portraits and painted material studies generated with OpenAI's built-in image generator for Sankofa. Fictional figures and theatrical landscapes, not verified portraits, dress or surveyed places. Cultural review pending. The project keeps prompts, output references and reviewed-byte hashes in the IllustratedTheatre, RiverThreshold and RiverCohesion art manifests.
