October 18–19, 2026 · Software evidence; independent AI reviews, not official scores or human acceptance

The user authorised a working MVP, repeated simulated judging and updated submission/documentation. The active requirement is [personal story table](../product/personal-story-table-mvp.md): personal curiosity must change an illustrated telling before its ending, with truthful historical/reconstruction boundaries, hands-accessible input and recovery, explicit local retention and a useful service-independent visit. The affected modules are core content, session, storage, service transport, XR presentation/placement and release tooling.

A limited-context reviewer read the actual implementation, public-synthetic provider outputs, current captures and official rubric, without prior panels. It assessed Innovation 6–7, Experience 5–7, Technical 5–7 and Polish 5–7 out of 10. These are uncertain AI assessments, not a prediction of placement.

Hardware, cultural/source, pronunciation/listening, participant response, asset terms, eligibility and release access remain external gates. They cannot be closed through repeated AI scoring. The reviewer found no new confirmed code P0 requiring the MVP to be discarded.

## Stabilization rule

Review the realised implementation after fixes, with fresh limited-context reviewers as well as a follow-up on prior findings. Two consecutive review rounds with no new actionable MVP P0/P1 findings constitute stabilization for this software iteration. Any unresolved new finding reopens implementation. Stable external gates are recorded as open; score increases are neither required nor evidence of acceptance. A stable panel may still judge the concept insufficiently differentiated.

No sound playback, device or real participant result is implied.

## Review chronology and convergence

Three limited-context reviewers covered experience, technical implementation and a fresh overall reading. They read official criteria and actual sources/captures/results, with no requested verdict. Initial provisional estimates were roughly 6–7/10 per criterion from the experience reviewer; the fresh reviewer gave a broad 57–75/100 range and the technical reviewer settled at 15–19/25 for Technical Implementation. Do not average these overlapping ranges or interpret them as official thresholds.

- Rounds 1–2 found missing question confirmation, long-detail/options layout, prose without payoff, late personal relevance, auth and local-error recovery issues. These drove actual changes.
- Round 3 identified the role-swap problem and smaller ending/capability/fire defects; the role issue was treated as actionable despite disagreement between P1/P2 severity.
- Round 4 read every new semantic sample and closed the concrete role-swap finding. No reviewer required broader coverage, live conversation or dynamic 3D to address the observed defects.
- Round 5 found stale setup logic suppressing network access and the gallery build record still presented as current. Setup migration and optional room feature configuration were fixed; the active candidate record now identifies StoryTable. Replay was also tightened to disallow new remote narration.
- Round 6: all three reviewers independently found no new actionable MVP P0/P1. Each checked the actual APK identity; the last minor wording inconsistency was aligned to the retained pair’s situations and outcomes.
- Round 7: all three reviewers again found no new actionable MVP P0/P1 or material evidence overclaim in the frozen source/artifact and final delivery wording. This meets the two-consecutive-round stabilization rule for this software iteration. All retained the human/device/release gates and the spatial-originality concern. No further implementation change followed these closing reviews.

The remaining competitive concern is stable: the writing is the principal entertainment, and the spatial advantage of the journal/fire/table has not been demonstrated with people. Different openings tend to converge on hospitality and shared water. Two mandatory context chapters may interrupt a small drama. Dense controls and loose illustration/prose correspondence remain P2 refinements. A stable software review does not establish exceptional originality or a likely prize.
