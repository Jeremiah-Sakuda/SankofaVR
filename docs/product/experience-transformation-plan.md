# Sankofa experience transformation plan

October 18, 2026. Proposed next product iteration, based on the [independent audit](../reviews/2026-10-18-independent-product-audit.md). This plan challenges the current scope; it does not silently replace the approved PRD, claim implementation, or authorize purchases, outreach or submission.

## The product bet

**Make Sankofa an intimate encounter you understand through what you hold.** Keep the painting as a recognizable beginning and ending. Make the exchange inside it the product's centre.

The first work should leave a visitor saying: “She handed me the gourd. I saw what it had cost her to bring it. Then I understood why the other woman waited.” That is a proposed outcome to test, not a quote from a participant.

The ambition is a precise piece of immersive direction: one object crosses from a painting into personal reach, connects two moments in time, and changes the meaning of an ordinary meeting. Exceptional voice, spatial continuity, character acknowledgement and a quiet return should make the experience memorable. A larger number of rooms, gestures or APIs is not the success measure.

Working public framing:

> **Sankofa — step inside the painting.**
> Discover the lives behind an unfinished exchange.

Supporting description: an imagined encounter grounded in a specific, reviewed history. The wider mission can remain bringing overlooked histories into view. Do not call the fictional meeting a recovered account, archival painting or the visitor's ancestry.

Primary audience hypothesis: curious adults who choose short films, illustrated stories or cultural experiences, including people unfamiliar with Kenya. The first product unit is a satisfying six-to-eight-minute encounter. A mass-market catalogue, educational efficacy, universal cultural coverage and ongoing retention are not yet demonstrated.

## Choose between these directions

| Direction | What it could add | Main risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extend and polish the museum | Atmosphere, browsing, future collection | More architecture/content without a stronger emotional reason to visit | Maintain current pavilion; no expansion in this iteration |
| A directed embodied short | Strong pacing, handoff and complete emotional arc | Familiar format depends on excellent performance | **Production fallback and control condition** |
| Object-led spatial memory | Discover a motive by handling an object that remains continuous across times | Becomes a clip menu or unreliable gesture; interrupts emotion | **Leading experiment**, time-boxed before commitment |
| Bounded conversational narrator | Personal questions during a scene | New speech/network/turn-taking infrastructure; weak benefit if answers are exposition | Defer; revisit only after repeated unmet visitor questions |
| Expressive shadow/puppet theatre | Continuous playful spatial action | New interaction language and script; substantial rewrite | Interesting separate direction, not this month's baseline |
| Paired visitors with partial accounts | Another person's perspective becomes socially necessary | Second headset, synchronization and a different operating model | Cheap later premise test; no multiplayer implementation now |

These alternatives came from independent reviewers and primary precedents summarized in the audit. The recommendation is not to combine them. If object-led memory fails to improve emotion and understanding, choose the directed short without treating that as a lesser product.

## What to preserve and what to reopen

Preserve Unity, the offline story core, deterministic flow, explicit action acceptance, cancellation, captions, one-hand alternatives, reduced motion, passive outcomes, local opt-in storage, and the no-AI-music constraint. Preserve the women's authority over their agreement. Preserve the possibility of watching quietly without blame or missing the fundamental story.

Reopen the advance perspective menu, separate ember onboarding, inset memory presentation, number of receive gestures, length of the spoken recap, save-gated continuation, and the assumption that a generalized griot voice is the right artistic framing for this setting. Keep the fire as a contextual presence if its performance earns that role; the title does not require a uniform voice for every future culture.

The current broader PRD still lists freeform generation, three plans and an additional language. Before implementation is declared a new baseline, update the PRD/scope/backlog together to distinguish the selected authored competition experience from those roadmap goals. Do not build a backend merely to make an old checklist appear complete.

## The decisive prototype

Build one **90-second excerpt** using existing figures, gourd, memory content and narration. It begins at the shortfall and ends with the reciprocal reason for waiting. No new pavilion art, full character rigs, second chapter or model integration.

Compare two versions:

- **Directed version:** the visitor receives or declines the gourd; a carefully staged memory unfolds around the same stationary seat; the present exchange resumes. Minimal extra input.
- **Object-led version:** the same story and staging, but a deliberate, forgiving action with the offered object initiates the memory. The object stays continuous between present and remembered scene. An explicit select alternative and passive version remain available.

The central spatial effect is the present and remembered meeting sharing a recognizable place and an object near the visitor. It is not a floating video window or a demand for a technically elaborate portal. Use the existing scene layers and staging first. No camera travel, mandatory arm extension, two-handed pose, precise wrist angle, continuous grip or rapid motion.

Choose the actual gesture after an on-device affordance probe. A preview may respond continuously, but only an explicit flow-owned commitment changes the telling. Merely attaching a route button to a gourd does not satisfy the experiment.

**Cap:** one 8–12-hour engineering spike, including one focused revision. If only a 40–60-hour total work envelope is available, cap the spike including revision at 4–6 hours and prefer the directed version. Do not hide new assets, integration or test work outside that allowance.

With six unfamiliar people, compare the two excerpts in counterbalanced order with matched content and finish. Ask for an unprompted retelling before asking whether they liked it. Record coaching, missed intentions, confusion, comfort and reasons for preference. A second exposure is not independent evidence of the story's surprise; account for that in interpretation.

Adopt object-led memory only if:

1. At least four of six produce and explain the intended action without coaching.
2. At least four describe the motive revealed, rather than only “I selected another scene.”
3. At least four prefer it for a concrete experiential reason; the directed version does not clearly produce better understanding or emotional connection.
4. The prototype preserves passive/one-hand completion and introduces no unresolved blocking or comfort defect.

These are directional gates, not statistical proof. If results are mixed, prefer the simpler directed version. If both versions fail to make the relationship interesting, rewrite the encounter before improving its visuals. If hardware is unavailable, screen/simulator comparisons can refine clarity but cannot pass the spatial/real-hand gate.

## Proposed first visit

The schedule below is a **6:15 creative target**, excluding optional questions, browsing and pauses. It is not a new timed test the visitor must satisfy. Every beat must work at a comfortable pace, and accessibility may legitimately extend it.

| Time | Experience | What should change for the visitor |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | One painting, one deliberate Enter action, concise disclosure and a hint of the unfinished exchange | A specific reason to enter before a menu of unfamiliar names |
| 0:25–0:55 | The composition opens. Wambui offers the gourd into reach; quietly watching leaves it nearby | An interpersonal invitation teaches the main gesture; remove the separate ember tutorial in this cut |
| 0:55–1:30 | Both women have legitimate needs; the shortfall becomes clear | Understand the conflict without cultural expertise or a villain |
| 1:30–2:20 | Wambui's limited portions appear as a spatial memory connected to the same object | Discover what coming to the meeting cost her |
| 2:20–3:00 | Naserian's earlier need, or a concise visible bridge to it, makes reciprocal patience clear | Her decision becomes understandable before the agreement |
| 3:00–3:45 | Visitor can help set the flour between them; Wambui acts on the passive path; Naserian makes the agreement | Participation changes inclusion without making the guest the moral authority |
| 3:45–4:40 | Hospitality: a clear turn toward the visitor, an offering, spatial sound and an unhurried pause | Feel included; this is the emotional peak |
| 4:40–5:20 | A short farewell and bead; reduce repeated instructions and receive waits | Something small can outlast the meeting |
| 5:20–5:55 | Return to the recognizable painting with the bead outside it; silence before at most one reflective line | Notice a changed understanding without being told the entire moral |
| 5:55–6:15 | Simple explicit Keep or Leave; optional next scene available without requiring a save | Closure before administration |

The two-motive structure is essential; the exact amount of each memory is experimental. One variant can emphasize one memory while giving a concise shared bridge for the other. Every complete first visit must earn the agreement without requiring an optional question or second visit. A later expanded perspective must add something beyond replaying the explanation already heard.

Retain regional specificity, the Maa name with reviewed delivery, ordinary material detail and appropriate humor. Shorter narration should create room for visible action and silence; it should not turn the setting into a generic parable. One warm narrator can voice the scene if the performance distinguishes the characters clearly. Separate actors are an option after listening evidence, not a required integration.

## Interaction and presentation contract

### One action language

Use receiving, inspecting and returning a meaningful object as a coherent sequence. A novice should not learn a new pose for every beat. Do not require a bead-saving gesture more complicated than the story action; keep a simple labelled alternative.

An optional memory has four explicit states: available, previewing, committed, ended/cancelled. The application owns the accepted result and permitted reveal. Presentation owns visual feedback. Tracking loss, pause, questions, timeout and early return must neither silently commit a choice nor strand an object. Carry continuity is preserved across the scene transform. Replay uses the committed state; it never guesses from the current hand position.

Test a slower-invitation setting or explicit “more time” control if anyone repeatedly tries but defaults. Passive hospitality is not a substitute for letting a person exercise their intended agency. A glance can acknowledge attention; it cannot consent to a memory, historical interpretation or saved data.

### Composition before texture count

The handoff, figures' expressions, gourd and memory reveal get the art budget. Correct silhouettes, registration, sightlines, scale, edge treatment and lighting before increasing resolution. Test binocularly at actual viewing distance. The current 2K atlas limits mean a larger generated source alone may not improve the runtime.

Remove the explanatory rectangle from the principal memory reveal. Place the earlier action in legible depth with a repeated spatial landmark. Keep primary action within the comfortable forward view; peripheral detail can supply presence without requiring head scanning.

Reduce plaque dominance through placement, hierarchy and context-sensitive detail. Keep Pause, Return and accessibility controls easy to discover and reach. The known layout failures are baseline defects to resolve, not a reason to hide controls. Captions must remain readable while the gourd is carried, with large text and either hand.

### Voice, sound and culture are production work

Review the complete performance at normal speed, including character distinction, pronunciation, pauses, ambience and object response. The current fire-positioned narration and ducked ambience provide a starting point; a spatial blend value is not a listening result. Silence and well-directed existing recordings may be more effective than adding a score. No AI-generated music.

Separate review responsibilities: historical/source review; Kikuyu and Maasai portrayal/material context; Maa/name pronunciation; and sound/performance direction. One family member cannot be presumed to cover every remit. Review the substantive encounter before final voice/art production. Track material objections and resulting changes, not just whether somebody “approved” a packet.

Choose one concrete, verified anchor from the existing source base—a regional exchange, material object or documented river history—and make its relationship to the invented meeting clear. Verify or narrow the still-unconfirmed flour-source claim. Do not introduce identifiable real people or family accounts without resolving the applicable content restrictions and rights. This is a content decision, not a new research platform.

## Return value without compulsory storage

Keep remains explicit, local and deletable. Exact replay preserves the recorded telling/actions under the existing declared replay limits; a new memory is labelled a new perspective.

Make the existing modern scene or an expanded memory available **during the current session without persisting a visit**. Use an ephemeral parent record for needed context; only Keep writes it. A returning session can restore only what was explicitly saved. Preserve legacy kept visits when package/route semantics change.

Test the existing shorter alternate before producing more content. The modern river can serve as an optional brief epilogue about the place's continuing life, clearly separating documented restoration from the imagined garden. It must not imply the fictional bead survived historically or manufacture descendants. If it feels like a second unrelated ending, shorten or defer it from the main showcase.

Do not promise a daily habit on the strength of a save button. Test whether people voluntarily return for an unanswered perspective within 48–72 hours. A satisfying standalone short with a worthwhile companion scene is a truthful product if broader repeat demand remains weak.

Weekly time and current headset access were asked during this audit but remain unconfirmed. Default scheduling assumes roughly 10–15 user hours/week and separately available assisted engineering capacity. Hardware remains an open dependency. No purchase or recruitment is presumed completed.

## Acceptance evidence

Use the six-person prototype comparison to choose direction, then **eight new participants** for the complete candidate. Mix familiarity with VR and with the story's context; include relevant one-hand/reach needs where recruitment permits. Report the actual sample and remaining coverage. Cultural reviewers have a different role from novice participants. None of these thresholds is an official judging rule or a market forecast.

| Question | Proposed pass signal | Response to failure |
|---|---|---|
| Can it explain itself? | At least 7/8 enter and complete the first intended action without coaching; target first action within 30 seconds after choosing Enter | Simplify the entry/affordance; do not add more wall text |
| Is there a compelling situation? | Early three-person opening probe before media production; final target at least 6/8 describe both women's immediate needs by 90 seconds | Rewrite/reorder before production if the early probe fails; reopen the affected passage if the final check regresses |
| Does the moment change understanding? | At least 6/8 retell the reciprocal reason for waiting and identify a specific moment that changed their interpretation | Improve performed evidence, or choose the simpler directed version |
| Is agency honest? | At least 7/8 distinguish helping/being included from deciding the bargain; no repeated pattern of visitors believing they changed actual history | Revise cues and claims; remove accidental moral framing |
| Does VR add value? | In matched comparisons, at least 4/6 identify a spatial/social benefit and prefer it for that reason; inspect comfort separately | One staging revision, then reduce mechanic scope; do not infer presence from pretty screenshots |
| Can people distinguish context and fiction? | At least 7/8 identify the invented women/meeting; material cultural/source objections explicitly resolved | Narrow or replace unsupported material; do not remove draft labels without acceptance |
| Are hands reliable? | At least 9/10 deliberate attempts per hand for each essential interaction, zero accidental state-changing actions; all participants have a successful accessible path | Fix the adapter/threshold or use a proven alternative; a small sample does not certify universal access |
| Do timing and recovery respect intent? | No repeated forced defaults while a visitor tries to act; pause, hand loss, recenter, return and save failure cannot corrupt ownership or outcome | Add time/choice control or simplify the interaction; stop new choreography |
| Is performance credible? | At least 20 minutes including two complete visits and transitions on supported hardware. No steady-state 10-second window below 60 fps; proposed quality target is p95 CPU/GPU within the selected refresh budget and fewer than 1% missed display frames. Record compositor data and thermal context | Simplify measured bottlenecks. At 72 Hz the frame budget is about 13.9 ms; adjust for the actual supported refresh. Averages or absent GPU values cannot pass the gate. These quality targets supplement the official minimum |
| Is it comfortable? | No unresolved reproducible discomfort from expansion, near objects/captions or posture; stop any session when discomfort occurs | Reduce motion, revise placement or make the gentler transition default; do not average away failures |
| Does another visit have value? | At least 3/8 voluntarily use the offered alternate within 48–72 hours, and can name new meaning | Treat it as an optional extra, not a retention claim; do not add rewards or compulsory saving |
| Can judges actually experience it? | Independent installation/access and complete offline hands journey from the exact release artifact | Release blocker; fix access before another visual pass |

Audio acceptance requires a later user-controlled or expressly authorized listening session. This planning turn did not play sound. Simulator checks remain useful for regressions and permitted footage, but do not close real-hand, binocular or sustained-device gates.

## The submission should prove one claim

Lead with the implemented signature: the object in a visitor's hand, the memory becoming spatial, and the exchange understood differently. Show the real cause and consequence in one readable sequence. Then establish the opening, passive/accessible path, return, and adaptation from the original web Sankofa. Simulator footage is labelled; edited footage is not presented as one uninterrupted visit. Generated concept video cannot stand in for the build.

A proposed 2:30 cut: 0:00–0:15 signature action; 0:15–0:35 premise/entry; 0:35–1:25 memory and changed understanding; 1:25–1:55 hospitality and return; 1:55–2:15 real alternatives/revisit; 2:15–2:30 adaptation and accurate availability. Adjust this to what is actually strongest after testing, not to an unbuilt storyboard.

Remain provisionally in **Adapted Entertainment**, with an honest before/after account. Show what carries over from web Sankofa and what was added in Unity. Recorded authored questions are not live AI; authored perspectives are not freeform generated stories. A fresh repo is not grounds to conceal lineage. Confirm final classification and rights through the appropriate sources rather than assuming them from this plan.

Before release, source, content version, APK, relevant test reports, footage and judge access must refer to the same candidate. Verify required notices and asset/provider rights, entrant/account eligibility, the Competition channel and external installation. Retain access and the submission freeze obligations. Existing private rules, credentials, raw review notes and builds stay excluded from Git and runtime packaging.

## Cuts and stop conditions

Cut first: pavilion expansion, extra gestures, decorative particles, additional exhibits, full rigs, blanket texture upgrades and redundant recap. Defer the coda from the main showcase if it competes with the ending. Cut the novel memory gesture if it does not beat the directed comparison.

Do not cut: a comprehensible motive for both women, truthful participation, accessible completion, usable pause/exit, actual listening, material cultural/source corrections, real device investigation and independent delivery checks.

No runtime world generation, general conversational backend, multiplayer, new platform migration, broad language rollout, catalogue creator tool or MR mode belongs to the critical path. They can be valuable later; none currently answers the most important uncertainty.

The next development assignment should therefore be **T1 plus the bounded T2/T3 proof**, followed by a product decision from the evidence. The deliverable is a stronger encounter, not another round of environment decoration.
