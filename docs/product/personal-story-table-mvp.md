# Sankofa personal story table MVP

October 18, 2026 · User-authorised direction · Integrated software MVP; device and human acceptance open

**Sankofa — For the stories you never got to hear.**

A place name, an inherited fragment, or a question can be enough to begin. A warm griot, present as a small fire, invites a seated visitor to explore an illustrated telling on a nearby table. Personal curiosity changes the thread of the story. The experience supplies historical context and openly imagined scenes; it does not recover ancestors, recreate a real relative or fill gaps in a biography as fact.

This is the active MVP scope. It supersedes the gallery as the primary journey while preserving [What We Carry and the earlier living gallery](illustrated-theatre-scope.md) as comparison work. The [PRD](prd.md), [delivery plan](delivery-plan.md) and [adaptation summary](adaptation.md) follow this direction. Previous gallery recordings, art, saved-visit formats and evidence remain historical records; their test results do not establish acceptance of the new table scene.

## The complete visit

| Moment | Visitor experience | Required behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Settle | A small fire and illustrated journal are close enough to use while seated | Start with an adjustable virtual stand; on compatible Quest hardware passthrough can retain the room |
| Bring a question | Choose a place, period and interest; optionally type a question or fragment | Hands-accessible keyboard and selection controls; no surname, microphone or identity inference required |
| Confirm | Read the selected context and decide between prepared and online creation | Explicit consent before sending selected context, question and fragment to the configured service |
| Follow the telling | Read illustrated chapters with a warm narrator's voice; each turned page's print rises in stereo depth like a pop-up book; on the headset the prepared voice reads by default beside full captions | Three pages on either route of a bundled telling, or a validated service telling; label generated versus bundled capability accurately |
| Choose a side | At the documented page, choose whose side of the meeting to follow, or turn the page and let the fire choose | The choice changes what is revealed, never a character's decision; the default is never recorded as the visitor's choice; the ending names the side still unheard |
| Ask nearby | Open a contextual question or source detail beside the current page | Bounded answers; return to the same chapter; disclose when the available context cannot answer |
| Close | Let the fire mark the ending, then choose whether to keep it | Saving is explicit; a new request is distinct from reopening saved realised content |
| Return | Open a kept telling or start another exploration | Exact stored text, illustrations and answers reopen without a new model request; deletion removes the retained telling |

The new scene is `Assets/Sankofa/Scenes/StoryTable.unity`, generated by `SankofaSceneBuilder.BuildStoryTable`. Its scene, input, persistence and placement adapters are integrated. Muted, caption-first operation is the current verification mode. Optional configured narration has a service adapter; actual playback and listening acceptance are not established by this work. There is no live microphone conversation.

## Published coverage and personalisation

| Coverage | MVP |
|---|---|
| Place | Enkare Nairobi, Kenya; canonical ID `enkare-nairobi` |
| Period | The 1850s, before the railway; canonical ID `1850s` |
| Interests | `journeys`, `trade-household`, `place-water` |
| Interface and story language | English |
| Historical evidence | Bounded C1 naming, C6 regional trade and C7 museum-vessel passages from the existing [source register](../content/enkare-nairobi/sources.md) |
| Images | Existing local `scenery-atlas`, `cast-atlas` and `modern-river` illustration IDs; the modern coda is an imagined comparison, not additional historical coverage |
| Cultural acceptance | Draft sourced context; family, community and pronunciation review remain pending |

A request outside this place or period returns an explicit unsupported result. It never becomes a generic African history or a nearby culture without the visitor choosing a supported context. Wider coverage requires its own sources, visual inventory and review.

The bundled composer has three authored tellings. Recognised themes in a question, then a fragment, can select a thread; otherwise the chosen interest controls it. The opening, historical development, illustration emphasis and contextual questions differ before the ending. It is labelled **bundled**, with no claim of fresh AI generation. Fragments guide what is explored; their raw text is not inserted into the bundled story. When the visitor wrote something, the first page states which recognised words chose the thread, or that they did not point to one; that explanation quotes only catalogue vocabulary and is not saved.

Each bundled telling offers two perspectives at its documented page: the person the opening follows, or the other person in the meeting. Both routes are three pages and end in a decision their own character makes. A kept telling stores both sides, so reopening it offers the choice again without generation. Generated service tellings remain linear.

The optional Google Gemini adapter requests new reconstructed scenes based on the selected interest and supplied question or fragment. The service adds exact historical passages and approved illustration IDs, validates the response and returns `mode: generated` only after a provider result is accepted. Missing configuration, timeout, cancellation or invalid output selects an explicitly bundled response or offers a retry. A deterministic fallback is not advertised as generation. Actual authenticated Unity requests with the same interest, empty fragment and two different public-synthetic questions produced distinct fictional situations and outcomes before the ending. This is narrow transport/content evidence, not general model acceptance.

## Truth and data boundaries

Historical passages must match the approved text and claim-specific source. A model's citation or evidence label cannot verify a new assertion. Imagined scenes begin with an explicit reconstruction disclosure. Visitor accounts, if retained in a future telling, require an unverified-account label and must never acquire historical status through a citation. The current composer and service omit a raw personal-account chapter.

Names and locations do not establish ethnicity, language, genealogy or a relative's actions. Generated characters are fictional; they must not be presented as recovered people. The application accepts validated data, never model-authored scene commands, scripts or arbitrary image URLs. Deterministic validation bounds the contract and catches known problems; it cannot independently prove every sentence of generated fiction appropriate or culturally sound.

Online consent concerns the selected region, period, interest, question and fragment. Room geometry stays in the placement adapter and is not sent to the story service. The service avoids request-content logging and persistent request storage; Google retention, applicable account terms and the full deployed path still need review before any stronger privacy promise. Do not put personal inputs, credentials, room captures or recordings in Git.

Generated narration is transient, so reopening a generated telling can be caption-only. Prepared recordings are local assets matched to exact text. Keep is a separate deliberate action. Reopening uses the realised story JSON, including its original capability label, approved art references, answers and sources. Room placement is not part of story history. Failed saving must remain visible and allow retry without silently claiming success.

## Why the table matters

The story has a physical place beside the visitor. A real table should change the journal's height and usable footprint; it is not merely a passthrough background. MR Utility Kit 207 integration reads locally available table surfaces after **Fit my table** is chosen. An explicit permission request follows Fit my table. The visitor can recenter or adjust the virtual stand when permission is denied, no suitable table is found, or room data is unavailable; permission/load waits are bounded. Deck axes remain aligned with the measured tabletop. A changed room must not prevent reading or reopening a telling.

This is implementation intent backed by an adapter, not verified room behaviour. Quest surface orientation, scale, reach, occlusion, passthrough and sustained rendering still need actual-device evidence. Full seated use remains possible through the manual stand.

## Deliberate deferrals

| Capability | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| Freeform voice requests, speech recognition and live conversation | Deferred; typed question/fragment and hands choices are the MVP path |
| Second narration/interface language | Deferred; English only; TTS language support does not establish conversation or cultural coverage |
| Dynamic image generation and arbitrary asset loading | Deferred; use the approved existing illustrations |
| Real-relative generation, ancestry recovery and genealogy services | Outside this MVP; no claims of recovered biography |
| Wider places and historical eras | Deferred until separately sourced and reviewed |
| AI-generated music | Excluded by the user |
| Gallery frame entry, tangible flour exchange and old continuations | Preserved comparison experiences; not requirements of the new table visit |
